How it works

20 lines pay left to right, starting on the first reel. Only the highest win on each line pays.

The 242 Wild lands on reels 2 to 5 and stands in for every symbol except Scatter and Coin.

3, 4 or 5 Scatters anywhere pay 2x, 10x or 50x your bet and award 8, 10 or 12 free spins. Every win in free spins is doubled, and free spins can retrigger.

Each 242 Coin carries a value. Land 5 or more anywhere and you collect all of them.
